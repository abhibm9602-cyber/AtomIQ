1. MULTILAYER HETEROSTRUCTURE MEMRISTORS (ITO/Al2O3/Au)
-------------------------------------------------------
- Device Structure: Indium Tin Oxide (ITO) / Aluminum Oxide (Al2O3) / Gold (Au) heterostructure.
- Tunneling Barrier Dynamics: The 1.6 nm ultra-thin Al2O3 tunneling barrier transitions from static quantum transport to dynamic filamentary switching under an applied electric field (>1.2 V/nm).
- Switching Mechanism: Oxygen vacancy migration leads to the formation of conductive filaments (CFs), switching the device between Low Resistance State (LRS) and High Resistance State (HRS).
- In-Memory Computing Relevance: The continuous modulation of conductance emulates biological synaptic weight updates, making ITO/Al2O3/Au memristors ideal for energy-efficient neuromorphic hardware accelerators.
- Density Functional Theory (DFT) Parameters:
  * Functional: GGA-PBE (Perdew-Burke-Ernzerhof) with Grimme D3 dispersion correction.
  * K-point grid: 6x6x1 for monolayer/surface relaxation; 12x12x1 for electronic density of states (DOS).
  * Cutoff Energy: 500 eV for wavefunctions.

2. PRECISION ATOMIC FREQUENCY STANDARDS & METROLOGY
--------------------------------------------------
- Atomic Clocks: Rubidium (Rb) optically pumped frequency standards operating at 6.834 GHz microwave transition (5S1/2 -> 5P3/2 optical pumping).
- Stability Metrics:
  * Allan Deviation (sigma_y(tau)): Characterizes frequency instability over averaging time tau.
  * White Frequency Noise: Slopes as tau^(-1/2) on log-log Allan variance plots.
  * Flicker Floor: Minimum stability point, typically reached between 10,000s to 100,000s averaging times.
  * Phase Noise (L(f)): Expressed in dBc/Hz at specified offset frequencies (e.g., 1 Hz, 10 Hz, 100 Hz from carrier).
